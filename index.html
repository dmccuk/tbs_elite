THE BLACK SHIP
Chapter 1: Lingering Systems
You are Warrant Officer Wyatt Staples, hauling bio-waste through hostile territory aboard the Space Refuse Collector MK-IV.

House Cayston has betrayed the Crown. Their forces hunt a Royal Yacht carrying a high-value VIP through the Lingering Systems.

You're just a garbage hauler with a black transponder. But sometimes, desperate times call for creative solutions…
Mission Objective
Protect the Royal Favor. Shoot down missiles and drones — then launch your cargo container at the corvette and blow it up when the ring turns green.

Mission Briefing

A House Cayston corvette is hunting the Royal Yacht Royal Favor. Your guns can't dent its armour — but they can shoot down its missiles and drones. Launch your bio-waste container at the corvette. When the ring around it turns green, time slows down: detonate! Three hits cripples it. Your compactor squeezes out a new container every 15 seconds — keep the yacht alive in between.
