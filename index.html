A patrol pilot, a garbage hauler, a royal yacht, and a warship that really should have picked on someone else.

  1. Prologue

    Before the waste hauler, Wyatt Staples flew patrol on the Tessick-Varn Frontier. Pirates have hit the Tessick-3 relay, and they've taken people.

    Now a Warrant Officer hauling bio-waste, Wyatt finds a royal yacht running from a House Cayston warship. His guns can't scratch it. His cargo might.
